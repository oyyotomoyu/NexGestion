# Barcode Scanning

## Overview

When recording items arriving at or leaving a warehouse (Inbound and Outbound in Inventory), you can scan a barcode instead of searching for the item by name. Each scan adds the matching item to the list, so receiving or shipping a delivery is faster and less likely to have the wrong item picked by mistake.

There are two ways to scan:

- A **barcode scanner device** connected to your computer.
- Your device's **camera** — a computer's webcam, or a phone/tablet's camera.

## Using a Barcode Scanner Device

If a barcode scanner is connected to your computer, no setup is needed — your computer treats it just like a keyboard. On the Inbound or Outbound screen, make sure the scan field shows "Scanner ready," then aim the scanner at the barcode and press its trigger. The matching item appears in the list automatically, and you can keep scanning additional items or additional units of the same item.

## Using Your Camera

- **On a computer**: click **Scan with Camera**. If your computer has more than one camera, choose which one to use, then hold the barcode in front of it.
- **On a phone or tablet**: tap **Scan with Camera** and allow the browser to use your camera when asked. The rear camera opens automatically, since that's the one you point at a barcode. Hold the barcode steady in the frame until it's recognized.

The camera turns off automatically as soon as a barcode is recognized, or when you stop scanning.

## Camera Scanning May Not Be Available

Some browsers only allow camera access when the app is reached through a secure address, and block it otherwise. If your organization's system isn't set up for secure access, the **Scan with Camera** option may not appear, or may show a permission error even after you allow access.

If that happens:

- use a barcode scanner device instead (Section above), which always works; or
- search for the item manually; or
- ask your administrator whether secure access can be set up.

## When a Barcode Doesn't Match

If a scanned barcode doesn't match any known item, the screen shows a "barcode not recognized" message instead of adding a line. The system never creates a new item automatically from a scan — use manual search to find the right item, or contact whoever manages your item catalog if it's genuinely missing.

## Where You Can Use This

Barcode scanning is currently available on the Inventory Inbound and Outbound recording screens.
